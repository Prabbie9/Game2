## Renderer decision (M0 prototype, shots/m0/proto_sheet.jpg)
- (a) WebGLRenderer + pmndrs postprocessing 6.36 (SSAO, bloom, AgX, SMAA): renders correctly on WebGL2.
- (b) WebGPURenderer + TSL (GTAO, bloom) via WebGL2 backend: also renders, needs `three`->`three/webgpu` alias, Sky->SkyMesh, separate build; TSL post API still moving between three releases.
- CHOICE: (a). Image quality was equivalent in the prototype; (a) is mature, WebGL2 is the laptop baseline, and (b)'s advantages (compute) need real WebGPU we cannot test. WebGPU-only features: behind a flag, untested. Not switching later.

## M2 look slice (architecture)
- Materials: src/materials/pbr.ts — KTX2 sets in assets/processed/tex/<slot>/{albedo,normal,orm}_{2k,1k}.ktx2 (tools/fetch_textures.py + tools/textures.py; ETC1S albedo/ORM, UASTC+RDO normals). High/Ultra load 2k, Medium/Low 1k. World-space shader patch: IQ anti-tiling, fbm grime, wet response + puddles + rain ripples (shared uniforms in WORLD_UNIFORMS).
- Buildings: src/world/buildings.ts — facades with real depth (piers/spandrels/recessed glass), cornice, parapet, rooftop clutter, shopfronts + awnings + signs, curtain-wall towers. Windows/shops use interior mapping (rooms, blinds, furniture silhouettes, shelves, lights at night) via emissive.
- Street: src/world/lookslice.ts — 16 m roads, 15 cm kerbs, paint, zebras, manholes/drains/tar, lamps (pooled lights), traffic lights (28 s cycle), leaf-card trees with wind, furniture, parked sourced cars (LOD1, frozen/merged).
- Reflections: SSR (src/renderer/ssr.ts) for wet up-facing surfaces (no G-buffer: depth + derivatives + world-space puddle mask); scene cube probe at the slice centre prefiltered by PMREM, re-baked when streaming settles and on TOD/weather change.
- Sky: Preetham + SkyDome overlay (fbm clouds lit by sun, stars, moon, city glow).
- Vehicles: tools/vehicle_prep.py (Blender): flatten, detect wheels (names/corners), orient +Z, scale, pivots, part names, LOD1, WebP 1024 textures, JSON meta -> src/vehicles/sourced.ts. First model: Kiri '86 (CC-BY, DanielZhabotinsky) replaces the procedural sedan (better look; passes the vehicle gate after geometry re-tune). Procedural CarVisual remains the fallback.
- Animation runtime: src/animation/clips.ts binds bundles by bare bone name and retargets the hips into the rig's parent space (handles rotated/cm armatures like three.js Michelle/Soldier).
- Shots wait for `pendingLoads()==0` before capture; shot mode renders only the final frames.

## M3 world generator + streaming
- Layout (src/world/citygen.ts, pure functions of the seed): Downtown 1.92 km square, 96 m blocks, 16 m streets, 24 m arterials (every 4th line), lots subdivided per block (towers near the centre, mid-rise outward), central plaza with fountain (set-piece), underpass trench on x-line -3 (6.5 m deep, 60 m ramps, covered tunnel |z|<56, cross streets bridge over), elevated highway (9 m deck, 20 m wide) above the z-line 4 arterial with 110 m end ramps. Districts plug in later (Harbour/Hillside in M8).
- Kit (src/world/kit.ts): geometry-only, worker-safe builders that append to a Batch keyed by material; colliders/light anchors/parked-car slots/traffic lights/steam vents emitted alongside.
- Streaming (src/world/streaming.ts): 128 m chunks; near ring (radius 1 chunk, 2 on Ultra) = full kit + physics colliders (physics worker groups) + pooled lights + parked cars + traffic lights; far ring (<= 6 chunks) = massing (one vertex-coloured window-grid material) + sidewalks; a citywide far ground plane (trench cut out) sits 3 cm under chunk ground. Two chunk workers, nearest-first queue, results applied time-sliced (~4 ms/frame), all kit materials pre-compiled with compileAsync at start. Unload with 2-chunk hysteresis. Camera collision uses near-chunk blocker AABBs. Reflection probe follows the player (re-bake after 160 m when streaming is idle).
- Traffic in M3 is a placeholder (bot cars on the highway and main arterial, wrap at the edges); lane-graph traffic is M7.

## M4 characters + animation (architecture)
- Physics (worker): src/sim/charsim.ts — kinematic capsule + Rapier KinematicCharacterController (autostep 0.36 m, snap 0.32 m, 50 deg climb limit, steep-ramp velocity projection, crouch capsule with stand-up headroom check), gravity/jump, per-foot ground rays (height + normal), forward obstacle probe (distance, top height, depth). src/sim/ragdoll.ts — 11 capsule segments, spherical joints (hinges with limits at knees/elbows), CCD, contacts off between joined parts.
- Animation: src/animation/animator.ts — locomotion blend tree over clip speeds (idle / walk / run / sprint, crouch set) driven by a shared gait phase (distance / stride) aligned on each clip's left-foot strike, so foot speed == ground speed and blends never cross legs; one full-body one-shot slot with fade envelope (jump, land, falling loop, vault/climb, sit/stand, drive, get-up), optional yaw stripping for turn clips.
- IK: src/ik/footik.ts — analytic two-bone legs, heel/toe contact with flush collapse of small authored lifts, foot raised to the higher of heel/toe ground (stairs, kerb edges), pelvis lowered to the lower foot, animated foot orientation preserved through the solve, ankle aligned to the surface normal (limited), planted-foot locking with 0.12 s release blend. Ground = analytic city height (citygen.groundAt) unless the physics foot ray disagrees (props, car roofs).
- Player: src/character/player.ts — camera-relative movement with inertia, facing turn rate by speed, walk/run/sprint/crouch, jump + falling + landing, vault (thin/low) or climb-on (<=1.3 m) or wall climb (<=2.25 m) from the probe, enter/exit car (route around the body to the driver door, sit while sliding in, hips pinned to the seat H-point, hands to the wheel rim by IK; reverse on exit), ragdoll + get-up. Camera: src/camera/follow.ts (orbit, shoulder offset, collision boom, recentre behind motion).
- Ragdoll controller: src/character/ragdoll.ts — segments measured off the live pose (works for any cast body), bones driven from the bodies, settle detection, get-up clip by face up/down, root aligned to the lying body by matching the clip's first-frame head direction, 0.4 s pose blend from the ragdoll snapshot.
- Crowd: src/crowds/vat.ts bakes LOD1 bodies over one walk cycle into float textures (positions + normals, 24 frames) at load; src/crowds/crowd.ts draws them instanced (per-instance phase, rate, clothing tint; shadow depth material shares the VAT fetch) walking block perimeters on the sidewalks from 26 m to 230 m around the camera.

## M5 weapons + combat (architecture)
- Data: src/data/weapons.json (damage, rpm, mag, reload, hip/aim spread, recoil, falloff, range, aim FOV, head multiplier, pellets, projectile, melee/throw params). In-game names are generic (Service Pistol, Compact SMG, Carbine, Pump Shotgun, Marksman Rifle, Launcher, Bat, Crowbar, Knife, Frag Grenade, Molotov).
- Models: tools/weapon_prep.mjs (orientation from geometry: longest axis forward, narrow end = muzzle, bore line up; real-length scale; sockets grip/support/muzzle/sight/eject/mag; WebP 512, meshopt) -> assets/processed/weapons. Loader + socket gizmos: src/weapons/models.ts.
- Rig: src/weapons/rig.ts — after the mixer: spine aim offset (yaw/pitch spread over three spine bones), weapon anchored at the right palm, aligned to the aim ray while aiming, long guns shouldered (stock to shoulder), right-hand IK + exact re-anchor, left hand IK to the foregrip (clamped to reach, iterated for palm offset) or the magazine during reload, recoil spring (kick + rise), reload tilt.
- Animation: armed stances (rifle, pistol) as directional gait sets (forward/back/left/right x speeds) in the same phase-synced blend; the player strafes facing the aim while aiming or just after firing.
- Combat: src/combat/combat.ts (trace vs world boxes + analytic ground, bone capsules by zone, vehicle OBBs; pellets; falloff; projectiles; throwables; explosions -> damage + worker impulses), src/combat/arsenal.ts (inventory, input, aim camera, fire/reload/melee/throw, crosshair + hit marker + ammo), src/fx/combatfx.ts (pooled effects).
- AI: src/ai/combatant.ts — perception (cone + LOS + hearing), cover from streamed boxes with reservation, grid routing, bursts with reaction delay and capped, distance-scaled error; fairness warning + grace; max 4 shooters; teams prevent friendly fire.
